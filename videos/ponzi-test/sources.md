## Not checked / uncertain (please note)
1. **The promise:** most sources say 50% in 45 days; some say 100% in 90 days or 50% in 90 days. We use "50% in 45 days" (the most cited). Smithsonian / Mass Moments could not be opened from this session (blocked) - checked via search results only.
2. **Money and investors:** estimates range from ~$10-15 million taken in and ~$15-20 million lost, and 20,000-40,000 investors. The script says "around fifteen million dollars" and avoids an investor count.
3. **160 million vs 27,000 coupons:** widely reported (Barron / the Boston Post); the attribution varies by source.
4. **"Almost none of it bought coupons":** based on accounts that very few coupons (reported ~$61 worth) were found; secondary sources.
5. **The line around the block:** crowds outside his office in July 1920 are widely described; exact scene is illustrative.
6. **Ponzi's look** (short, straw boater, cane, moustache) comes from popular descriptions/photos - used only as signature traits in our style.
7. Plea date differs between sources (Nov 1 vs Nov 30, 1920) - the script gives no date.
