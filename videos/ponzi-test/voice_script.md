# Voice script - 50% in 45 Days

## NARRATOR (ElevenLabs) - record in this order, one file, a short pause between lines
Delivery: calm, low, true-crime; slight smile on the Pulitzer line; slower on the final question.

1. Boston, 1920. A line of people wraps around the block.
2. They're waiting to hand a stranger their savings.
3. Fifty percent profit in forty-five days.
4. His name became the word for this scam. The trick still hunts your money today.
5. Charles Ponzi said he'd found a loophole in the mail.
6. Buy postal reply coupons cheap in Europe. Cash them in America for more.
7. Around fifteen million dollars poured in.
8. But here's the strange part. Almost none of it bought coupons.
9. His plan needed 160 million coupons. About 27,000 existed.
10. Then the Boston Post started asking questions. It all collapsed.
11. Ponzi pleaded guilty to mail fraud. The newspaper won a Pulitzer.
12. That was his problem. Here's yours.
13. The SEC's red flags: guaranteed high returns. Returns that never dip. A seller who isn't registered.
14. Before you invest, look them up at investor dot gov.
15. A century later, the line is still there. It just moved online.
16. Would you have stood in that line?

- INVESTOR (voice: worker): nervous - "So where's my money right now?"
