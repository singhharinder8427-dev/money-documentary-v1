# 50% in 45 Days - test script (~1 min)

**Alternative titles:** The Coupon King · The Line Around the Block · The Original Scam

**Angle:** follow the con man, then turn to the viewer's wallet. **Motif:** the line of people (opens and closes the video).

**Length:** 174 words ≈ 67 s of speech, ≈ 80 s with title card, 2 chapter cards and gaps.

## HOOK
- **NARRATOR:** Boston, 1920. A line of people wraps around the block.
- **NARRATOR:** They're waiting to hand a stranger their savings.
- **NARRATOR:** Fifty percent profit in forty-five days. [CARD]
- **NARRATOR:** His name became the word for this scam. The trick still hunts your money today.

**[TITLE CARD: "50% IN 45 DAYS" - gold "50%" + sound logo]**
## CHAPTER 1 - THE LOOPHOLE
- **NARRATOR:** Charles Ponzi said he'd found a loophole in the mail.
- **NARRATOR:** Buy postal reply coupons cheap in Europe. Cash them in America for more.
- **INVESTOR:** So where's my money right now? (dramatised)
- **PONZI:** Working. In coupons. Come back in forty-five days. (dramatised)
- **NARRATOR:** Around fifteen million dollars poured in. [COUNTER 0→15,000,000 gold]
- **NARRATOR:** But here's the strange part. Almost none of it bought coupons.
## MIDPOINT TWIST
- **NARRATOR:** His plan needed 160 million coupons. About 27,000 existed. [CARD]
## THE FALL
- **NARRATOR:** Then the Boston Post started asking questions. It all collapsed.
- **NARRATOR:** Ponzi pleaded guilty to mail fraud. The newspaper won a Pulitzer.
## CHAPTER 5 - YOUR WALLET
- **NARRATOR:** That was his problem. Here's yours.
- **NARRATOR:** The SEC's red flags: guaranteed high returns. Returns that never dip. A seller who isn't registered.
- **NARRATOR:** Before you invest, look them up at investor dot gov.
## CALLBACK ENDING
- **NARRATOR:** A century later, the line is still there. It just moved online.
- **NARRATOR:** Would you have stood in that line? [CARD]
