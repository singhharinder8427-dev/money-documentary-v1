## CH1 - The Memo

- **NARRATOR:** Remember what we know. She has not been charged. Beijing calls it groundless. The Justice Department has reached no conclusion. [CARD]
- **NARRATOR:** The memo, reportedly, was meant to help officials who deal with Huang know what they were dealing with.

## CH4 - I Haven't Heard That
- **NARRATOR:** Then it all came out within days.

- **NARRATOR:** November ninth. China's Foreign Ministry calls the spy claim groundless. The President says Huang told him by phone he had no idea.
- **NARRATOR:** November twelfth. Huang resigns. Democrats Neal and Wyden write to Lutnick, saying the administration did not appear to take the threat seriously.
- **NARRATOR:** Congress wants answers. House chairs asked for a closed-door briefing. Huang agreed to testify before Thanksgiving.
- **NARRATOR:** Monday the sixteenth. The President says he has not heard about Rubio's warning. He says he would be fine with Huang coming back.

## CH5 - Your Wallet
- **NARRATOR:** Here is where this reaches your money.
