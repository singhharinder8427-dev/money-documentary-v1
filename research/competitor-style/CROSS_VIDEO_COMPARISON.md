## Top 10 techniques ranked by likely contribution

1. **Make the thesis a physical object.** In AI, the size contrast between the character and the complete balloon reveals the premise at 00:03.4–00:05.0.
2. **Alternate visual formats as the question changes.** Spy uses a dinner, map, black card and close-up in 00:00–00:07. Each supplies a different kind of information.
3. **Light the place the eye should go.** At Spy 00:00 and Crash 00:04.0, the green spill unifies the subject with the information it carries.
4. **Use brief black phrases to mark a turn.** Jensen takes the screen from high color and visual scale to a single sentence at 00:01.43–00:04.07.
5. **Sequence quantitative change.** Jensen's year tags and tower or Crash's settling loss counter convert magnitude into an action with a final read.
6. **Change the framing when the stakes become personal.** Crash's chip fills more of the top edge and the face gets larger around 00:06.
7. **Build depth with overlap and scale cues.** Jensen's airplane, character and skyline split the opening into three readable distances at 00:00–00:01.4.
8. **Put micro-actions in held frames.** Spy's fork and mouth shape change at 00:06.2–00:07.8 while set lighting and composition stay legible.
9. **Repeat motifs in a changed state.** AI's initial cyan bubble and damaged later version around 25:10–26:40 provide a visible before and after.
10. **Use a short whip to carry momentum across space.** Spy 00:02.0–00:02.4 and AI 00:08.6–00:09.0 show brief directional smear between readable endpoints.

The ranking is editorial judgment from the viewed material. Audience retention curves were unavailable.
